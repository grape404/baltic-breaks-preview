LISBON is the capital of Portugal. Very conveniently located in Lisbon Coast, the actual 13 Golf Courses distinguish themselves due to their natural beauty and challenging but friendly design. There are nine 18-hole courses and four 9-hole courses. Most of these courses were designed by internationally renowned architects.

There is a wide choice of courses of different styles and difficulty level of play. The climate is mild and temperate, allowing golf to be played all year round. Quinta da Marinha, set amongst beautiful pine trees and stretching down to Guincho beach, has some of the most beautiful scenery in Portugal. It is a picture postcard of Portuguese golf!

The Hotel Quinta da Marinha Resort is the ideal place to close major business, play golf and live unforgettable moments, while finding luxury and comfort in the outdoors. Here you will find everything only a 5-star hotel can offer. To constantly experience new feelings.
