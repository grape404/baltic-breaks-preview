MARRAKECH GOLF FESTIVAL 2025 International Amateur Golf Week Featuring: LE NAOURA MARRAKECH GOLF CHALLENGE 2025 JAAL RIAD RESORT GOLF CUP 2025 PICKALBATROS MARRAKECH GOLF TROPHY 2025 MOVENPICK GOLF CUP 2025 From 9 to 16 November 2025 MARRAKECH - MOROCCO Introduction The Marrakech Golf Festival will be played on some of the most fabulous golf courses in MARRAKECH (ASSOUFID GOLF CLUB + AKENZA GOLF CLUB + PALMGOLF OURIKA GOLF CLUB + NORIA GOLF CLUB + PALMERAIE GOLF CLUB) - This event is open to amateur golfers of all levels (Men + Ladies + Senior men + Senior Ladies) FORMAT Individual Stableford.

Full handicap WHS with respective course adjustments. Yellow (men) and red (ladies) tees will be used. CLASSIFICATION CATEGORIES IN EACH EVENT General Gross Net Men Category A – handicap 0.0 to 16 (WHS)* Net Men Category B – handicap 16.1 to 36.0 (WHS)* Net Ladies – 0.0 to 36.0 (EGA) HCP playing limit per field: 28 for men and 36 for women There are no accumulated prizes, Gross will always have priority over Net.

In the case of a tombola, all players participate. PERMANENT APPLICATION RULES: 1- It is permitted to place a ball on the fairway (max distance a score card). once replaced the ball is in play 2- relief of roots throughout the cut grass area 3- when the game ball is out of bounds the player may choose to drop a ball on the fairway with two strokes and a penalty.

The reference point is the point where the ball crosses the edge of the fairway, counting two club-lengths of relief from there. 4- in each formation the player with the lowest handicap will be the team captain, being responsible for reminding his playing partners of the pace of play marked on the cards, which on all courses is expected to be 4h30 The tournament director holds the right to adjust the playing handicap during the event if he decides to do so This tournament week is great fun and an enjoyable way of making new friends, friendly competition and play on some outstanding golf courses in Morocco as well as to discover the pearl of Morocco = the magical city of MARRAKECH Tournament director: Mr.
