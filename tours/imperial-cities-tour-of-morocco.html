Thanks to the MNTO 7 Nights / 8 days Tour departing from Marrakech Programme Highlights: Day 1: Arrival to Marrakech and transfer to your hotel Day 2: Marrakech (Whole Day discovery of Marrakech) Day 3: Marrakech – Casablanca Day 4: Casablanca – Rabat Day 5: Rabat – Meknès – Volubilis - Fez Day 6: Fez Day 7: Fez - Marrakech Day 8: Departure: Transfer to Marrakech Airport / End of our services Day 1: Arrival to Marrakech Meet & Greet at Marrakech Airport and private transfer to your hotel in Marrakech.

Check-in and Overnight at your hotel. Day 2: Marrakech (Visit of Marrakech) After Breakfast at the Hotel – meeting with the Tour Guide for a full day guided city tour of Marrakech Visit of the Koutoubia mosque, Palais Bahia, Saadien Tombs and the Majorelle Gardens, Old Town (Medina) – Souks and the famous DEJEMAA EL FNA SQUARE (Lunch at leisure not included in the price) Day 3: Marrakech – Casablanca After breakfast at the hotel, transfer via the highway to Casablanca.

Upon arrival to this city, which is the largest city of Morocco, half day guided tour of Casablanca. The visit will take you to the main touristic attraction of the economic capital of Morocco including, Bab Marrakech, Aïn Diab Corniche, central market, Habous quarter, and a panoramic visit of the huge Hassan II mosque (from outside).

End of the visit and transfer to your hotel. Check-in and free time to rest. Day 4: Casablanca – Rabat After breakfast at the hotel, departure to Rabat. Half day Guided Tour of the royal city of Rabat including the royal mausoleum, Hassan tower, the ancient necropolis site of Chellah and the fortress of Ouddayas. End of the visit and transfer to your hotel.

Check-in and free time to rest. Day 5: Rabat – Meknes - Volubilis – Fez After breakfast at hotel, departure to FEZ via VOLUBILIS and the small holy town of Moulay Idriss. Then Stop at Meknes to visit the main touristic features such as Bab Mansour and the old Medina. Upon arrival to FEZ city, your guide will give you a Brief introduction of this magnificent city.

Transfer to your hotel and check in & overnight. Day 6: Fez (Exploration Day) After breakfast, Full day guided city tour of FEZ. The exploration will start at the tanners situated before the Andalusian quarter. The walk continues to Ess-Seffarine Square, the Moulay Idriss tomb which is the most visited and revered attraction in FEZ.

Then visit of the El-Nejjarine square and the cabinet makers’ souk and then visit the vast Bou Inania Medersa and Bab Boujloud. (Lunch at leisure not included in the price) Transfer back to your Hotel after the Tour.
