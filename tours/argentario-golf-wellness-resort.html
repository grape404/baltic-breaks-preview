The Argentario Golf & Wellness Resort is a 5-star resort with design rooms and villas, a PGA National Golf Course Italy with 18 holes, a wellness center and two restaurants. Experience luxury and design in an original and unique way in this exclusive location in the fascinating Maremma Tuscany. Located on the Monte Argentario promontory in the Maremma region of south west Tuscany just 90 minutes from the Eternal City, Recognised as one of Italy’s most luxurious golf properties, the Resort was rebranded earlier in the year to the Argentario Golf & Wellness Resort to reflect more accurately the importance the Resort places on the wellbeing, both mental and physical, of its guests.

Meanwhile as part of The PGA Branded Properties elite group of iconic golf properties, Argentario is keen to showcase its excellent PGA National Golf Course, Trackman equipped practice facilities and club fitting services. In addition, as we offering Ryder Cup packages or golf tours to the Marco Simone Golf Club, host of the Ryder Cup matches in September 29 to October 1, 2023, the Resort is a great base from which to play the Marco Simone layout and a number of other courses in the region as well of course, the PGA National Italy championship course at Argentario.

The luxurious Argentario Golf & Wellness Resort, located in the relatively undiscovered region of the Maremma, is the ideal destination for golfers looking for relaxing holiday that combines golf, wellness and gastronomy surrounded by nature. Set on the Monte Argentario promontory, the Resort is home to a course that meanders through cork-oak woods and olive groves and boasts incredible panoramas over the Orbetello Lagoon and Tyrrhenian Sea.

The eco-friendly layout has been awarded ‘BioAgriCert’ and is a GEO-certified course that participates in an ‘on course’ programme to continuously improve sustainability. The hotel itself combines drama, design, style and comfort; a majestic entrance, an imposing central stairway and gigantic windows are in stark contrast to the intense blue Tuscan sky and Mediterranean landscapes whilst the 73 rooms and suites offer sleek, modern interiors that are beautifully appointed, all with private decked terraces.

The Resort also offers accommodation in its Argentario Golf Villas which are bespoke luxury villas boasting ground-breaking design and stunning views of the lagoon, sea and golf course that blend seamlessly into the landscape. Meanwhile an extensive spa, wellness centre and two swimming pools (one outdoor and one indoor) alongside two tennis courts, two padel courts, a mini football pitch, an outdoor fitness station and a series of running trails means that there is something for everyone.
