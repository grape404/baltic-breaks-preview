10 nights Tanjung Benoa – Grand Mirage Hotel or Nikko Beach Hotel Based on Shanti Mandala Hotel – Ubud & Grand Mirage Tanjung Benoa 22 Novenber 2025 : Transfer in with English Speaking Guide Apt – Ubud, overnight Shanti Mandala Ubud. 23 Nov ’23: Kintamani and Barong Dance , Towards the Volcano ( 8 hours ) One of the most exciting full day trips you can make in Bali.

Your tour begins with a Barong & Kris dance, a fascinating dance drama about the eternal battle between good and evil. The next stop is a visit of handicraft villages of woodcarvers ( Mas ) & silver and goldsmith (Celuk ). Then on to Goa Gajah, an " Elephant Cave " dating back to the 11th century. Afterwards enjoy the spectacular panorama of Mount & Lake Batur from the mountain town of Penelokan - Kintamani.

On the way back you make a final halt at Tampaksiring, location of spectacular royal tombs and holy springs site. Lunch included Departure from your hotel at 8.30am 24 Nov 2025: E-bike at Mambal – Incl Lunch On this 2 hour cycling trip with electric bikes south of the busy centre of Ubud you ride through small, quaint villages where you see the unique Subak irrigation system at work, go past rivers and ride through its rustic, lush surroundings, guided by one of our experienced local guides.

While cycling you will see gorgeous rice paddies with local farmers at work, dams & waterfalls, we make a stop at the Ayung river, pass by Bali Green School... Following your cycling trip a tasty traditional Balinese meal is served in an authentic Balinese compound. Highlights. • Explore the un-touristy part South of Ubud with a mountain bike • Cycle through Bali countryside and villages along the rice and flower fields • Ride your bike alongside the Ayung river on the old river banks built by the Dutch • Lunch at a traditional Balinese compound 27 Nov ’23: Transfer Ubud – Nusa Dua with English speaking guide.

Overnight at Nikko Beach Hotel , 9 nights 27 Nov – 06 Dec 2023 : Nikko Beach Hotel , 9 nights 28 Nov ’25 : Jatiluwih / Bedugul ,The Wide Open Countryside ( 8 hours ) Today you will encounter Bali magnificent terraced rice fields, highland life and plenty of idyllic views. This popular tour start with a visit to Bali's Butterfly Park.

At Jatiluwih you will find the most scenic rice terraces on the island, and a bit further up slope a gorgeous hidden jungle temple can also be visited. At Bedugul with its climate you will first visit the fruit market, and then Beratan crater lake with its dramatic water temple. Lunch included. Departure from your hotel at 8.30
