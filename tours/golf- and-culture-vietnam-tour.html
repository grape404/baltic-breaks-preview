Welcome to Vietnam and Hanoi! You are met on arrival at the Hanoi airport, and transferred to your hotel. Blending the modern with the mystery and charm of past centuries, Hanoi offers an air of elegance and harmony with nature. Start the afternoon with a half-day sightseeing: visit to the Van Mieu or Temple of Literature.

The temple dates back to 1070, and is dedicated to the cult of Confucius. Note the peaceful series of walled courtyards and graceful gateways; this is one of the best examples of traditional Vietnamese architecture. Next, continue to the Ho Chi Minh Memorial Complex, containing a museum and Ho Chi Minh's final resting place.

See the Ho Chi Minh's Stilt House, the simple two-room dwelling where he lived from 1958 until his final days in 1969. Nearby, sitting in a lotus pond, view the miniature One Pillar Pagoda, or Chua Mot Cot. Made out of a single large tree trunk, it was originally constructed in 1040, and then rebuilt after destroyed by the French in 1954.

Next, enjoy a cyclo tour through Hanoi's Old Quarter - the oldest continuously developed area of Vietnam, highlighting more than 1000 years of history. Explore the network of narrow alleyways known as 'The 36 Streets." This is a wonderful place to watch the local life and purchase items from fabrics to crafts, to exotic medicines.

Each street was named to reflect the specialized trade of the merchants - such as silk traders, jewelry, etc. Overnight in Hanoi. Day 2: Hanoi Golf (B) After breakfast, transfer to Hanoi Kings Island Golf Club for a 18 holes. Kings Island Golf Club is a 36-hole golf resort in the outskirts of Hanoi and one of the most scenic courses in the North of Vietnam.

It lies on the shores of the Dong Mo reservoir, 40km from Hanoi. You access to the court by boat. There are two 18-hole courses: the lakeside course opened in 1993 and the Mountainview course opened in 2004. Back to Hanoi. Overnight in Hanoi. Day 3: Hanoi to Halong (BLD) Today, drive to Halong Bay, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and a 1500 km2 area of picturesque islands shrouded in mist, floating villages and pearl fisheries.

Embark on the boat for a relaxing excursion aboard a traditional Chinese-style wooden junk. Cruise on the emerald waters between dolomite islets and some of the 3,000 craggy limestone cliffs dotted with breathtaking caves and grottos. Visit popular sites such as Dau Nguoi (Head Island), Con Rua (Turtle Island) and Cong Troi "Heaven's Gate Island" on the way to the Luon cave to view the beauty of the surreal and subterranean world of stalagmites and stalactites.

Stop at Titop Island. Enjoy swimming, relaxing on the beach and climbing the steps to the top of the mountain on the island for fantastic views and photo opportunities. A wonderful seafood lunch and dinner is included during your cruise of this mystical bay. Anchor in the middle of nowhere for the night. Overnight on the boat.
