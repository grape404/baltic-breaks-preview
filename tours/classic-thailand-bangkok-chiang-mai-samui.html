This package offers 3 of the most visited destinations of Thailand. Start your journey in Bangkok, the capitalcity and the focal point of Thailand, to visit iconic temples and the Grand Palaceas well as the renowned floating market at Damnoen Saduak. There is room for endless entertainment opportunities in the city with excellent choice of eating options.Then fly to Chiang Mai, the latest capital of Lanna Kingdom before it’s merged into Siam.Our package hascompiled a hand-picked list of highlights that you can see all the most important landmarks includingWat Phrathat Doi Suthep.

We also visit hill tribe village and joinelephant care program; where you can be closer to the big gentle friends.Finish off at Samuiand get your skin tanned under the deep blue sky, feel the sand between your toes while listening to the gentle lapping of waves peacefully washing ashore. Koh Samui is lush with tropical jungle, its shore lined with nodding coconut palm trees and surrounded by aquamarine sea.

Most beaches offer peace andrelaxation yet some come up with lively nightlife, and choices of restaurants and bars. Day 1: Arrive in Bangkok . Be welcomed and greeted by our local guide. Transfer to your hotel and the rest of the day is at your own leisure. Approximate total distances:Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK) to Bangkok city centre: 36 km (50 minutes) Meals included: None Accommodation: Option 1Shangri-La Bangkok –Shangri-La Wing Deluxe Room Option 2 Mandarin Oriental Bangkok –Deluxe Room**Triple sharing not allowed Breakfast at the hotel.Take a tour that included three of Bangkok’s most famous temples, plus the iconic Grand Palace.

The first on the tour; Wat Trimita 700-year-old, three-meter tall golden statue, cast from five-and-a-half tons of solid gold which makes it the world's largest solid gold statue. Wat Phofeatures one of the largest reclining Buddha images in Thailand. It is also known as the birthplace of traditional Thai massage. The Temple of the Emerald Buddha, situated in the Grand Palace complex, otherwise known as Wat Phra Kaewis regarded as the most sacred temple in the kingdom.

It is home to the Emerald Buddha, a dark green statue which has been carved from a single jade stone. The former residence of the King, the Grand Palace, adjoins the temple. Note:Dress code for the Grand Palace & Wat Phra Kaew: Men must wear long pants and shirts with sleeves (no tank tops. If you are wearing sandals or flip-flops you must wear socks (no bare feet.) Women must be similarly modestly dressed.

Skirts need to fully cover the legs, no see-through clothes, no bare shoulders, etc.Return to the hotel.Rest of the day is at your own leisure .Approximate pick-up & drop off times:08:00 –12:30 hrs. Approximate total distances:Bangkok city centre to Grand Palace 12 km. / 35 minutes
