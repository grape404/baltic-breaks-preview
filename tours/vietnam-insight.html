Day 1 Arrive Ha Noi (D) Arrive in Ha Noi. Meet your Salute Vietnam Travel guide and transfer to hotel. Following by a water puppet show in the evening after very tasteful dinner with special dishes of Vietnam Overnight in Hanoi Day 2 Ha noi - Ha Long Cruise 1night 2days (BLD) Enjoy the 170km journey through the rich farmlands of the Red River Delta to Ha Long.

On arrival, transfer to the nearby port, board the boat cruise at about 12:00 and have your seafood lunch on board. Considered Viet Nam’s greatest natural wonder, Ha Long Bay boasts over 3,000 islands that jut out of the sea to provide a spectacular natural environment. We have a chance to stop at the Tuan Chau Island to make a short exploration of this Island and swim at the deserted beach of Titop Islet.

It is a time in the evening to enjoy sunset or fishing opportunity while having dinner. Overnight in boat (deluxe cabin with AC) Day 3 Ha Long - Cat Ba (B) After breakfast the cruise ends at Cat Ba island, one of Viet Nam's most famous national park. We stay on the island, explore the nature trails, with the rest of the day enjoy the island’s many unspoiled beaches .

Overnight in CAtba Island Day 4 Cat Ba - Ha Noi – Sa Pa (B) Heading back to the mainland by an afternoon hydrofoil to Hai Phong. A cyclo tour along the wide boulevards include a visit to the Du Hang Pagoda, founded over three centuries ago. Returning to Ha Noi by road and transfer to Ha Noi ontime to board the night train to the Hillstation of Sa Pa.

Overnight on train (first class with AC) Day 5 Sa Pa (B) Arriving in Lao Cai and transfer further to Sa Pa Hillstation. This area is home to the H'mong and Red Dzao, who are among Vietnam’s most colorful minority groups. From here we walk through the Ham Rong Mountains, visit H'mong people in the Cat Cat village, wander in the market bustling with hill tribe traders and witness the ancient tradition of the Red Dzao people - Their “Love Fair” held in every saturday night (if possible) Overnight in Sapa Day 6 Sa Pa – Lao Cai - Ha Noi (B) To day we trek deeper to meet Red Dzao people in the Ta Van village before getting on the same carriage for our overnight return to Hanoi.

Overnight on train (first class with AC) Day 7 Ha Noi (B) Arrive Hanoi in the early morning. Enjoy special “Hanoi Noodle” (PHO) for breakfast! Time for relaxing before starting your Ha Noi city tour. This is a city of lake, shaded bouleveards, verdant public parks and French colonia architecture. The city tour includes such sight as Ho Chi Minh's mausoleum, the one pillar pagoda, historic museum, the temple of literature, the lake of Restored Sword and Old quarter.

Overnight in Hanoi Day 8 Hanoi – Hue (B) Free time until we get the flight to Hue ancient city. From the center of Hue, a boat trip on the romantically named Perfume Pagoda takes us to Hue's best-known religious site, Thien Mu pagoda, and the mausoleum of former emperor Minh Mang, in a tranquil setting of garden and lakes.

Enjoy a royal dinner before come on board for a night performance of traditional music and songs of Hue Overnight in Hue
