8 Days 7 Nights 4 Rounds - Cambodia Golf Experience Golfers & Non-Golfers: Arrival to Siem Reap, Transfer to hotel for check in Golfers: Golf at Phokeethra Country Club, designed by V-Golf Non-Golfers: Visit the School of Fine Art "Les Artisans d'Angkor" of the National Silk Centre, a fascinating way to learn about Cambodian Silk.

Continue to Angjet Angjom, the famous Buddhist temple in Siem Reap and drive thru the Royal Palace. The rest of the afternoon, will be dedicate for shopping at Old Market area. Golfers: Golf at Siem Reap Booyoung Country Club, designed by Kentaro Sato Non-Golfers: After breakfast at hotel, visit Angkor Thom that includes the South Gate of Angkor Thom, Bayon Temple, Terrace of Leper King and Terrace of Elephant.

Continue to Ta Prohm temple a.k.a roots temple that popularized by Angelina Jolie in Tomb Raider movie. Golfers & Non-Golfers: Visiting the World's Wonder, the Angkor Wat. Overnight at your hotel. Golfers: Golf at Angkor Golf Resort, an award winning golf course designed by European legend, Sir Nick Faldo
