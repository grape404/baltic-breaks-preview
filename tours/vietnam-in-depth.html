DAY 1 : ARRIVAL TO HANOI ( - / Dinner) Airport pick up. Tour in Hanoi Old Quarter The Old Quarter of Hanoi is referred to as the city of ancient 36 streets and districts. These streets represented professional guilds of the ancient Vietnam Capital that we can even find nowadays. Traveling from the hotel, we pass many French influences including Hanoi Opera House, the State Bank and the former Governor's palace.

Afterwards, we journey around beautiful Hoan Kiem Lake, tour the Hanoi Cathedral built in late 19th century. Finally, we wander the lively old streets filled with traditional crafts, having some great bargain before we return to the hotel. Enjoy dinner with special cuisine in local restaurant Overnight in Hanoi DAY 2 : HANOI - NINH BINH (Breakfast / - ) Depart Hanoi for Hoalu by car.

The picturesque site of Hoa Lu was one the ancient capital of Vietnam during the Dinh, Le (10th century) and Ly (11th century) dynasties. Upon arriving, we explore two temples, one dedicated to King Dinh Tien Hoang and the other to his successor Le Dai Hanh. It is also a chance to visit the scenic Bich Dong Pagoda from 1428 as we have a leisurely boat ride to the three magnificent grottoes of Tam Coc, a Ha Long on Land called by many travellers.

Overnight in Ninh Binh. DAY 3 : NINH BINH – HA LONG (Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner) Car transfer to Halong. Check in board. A cruise in a private junk on the emerald green waters among the 3,000 spectacular limestone islands begins with fresh seafood lunch on board. Return to Hanoi by road in the late afternoon with a stop at Dong Trieu ceramic village.

DAY 4 : HA LONG – HANOI / HUE (Brunch / - ) Disembark around noon, transfer to Hanoi for flight to Hue in the evening. Overnight in Hue DAY 5 : HUE (Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner ) Taking a Dragon Boat, we travel along the serene Perfume River, visiting the sublime Thien Mu Pagoda built in 1601, which become symbolic of Hue’s peaceful beauty and was classified as the 14th on the list of the Imperial Capital’s 20 landmarks.

Travelling through pine forest and lush hills about 7Km southwest of Hue, we reach the mausoleum of King Tu Duc, a miniature of the Royal Palace, which harmonizes perfectly with the nature surroundings. On the return, we visit the King Khai Dinh’s Tomb with completely different architectures from any of other Nguyen King’s Tombs.

This burial place combines aspects of both Vietnamese and French influences, including the decorative styles of each culture. Continue to visit Royal Citadel where the ruling Nguyen Dynasty and Royal Families used to live as we explore its many fascinating architectural remainders that survived after the wars. Enjoy the royal dinner at local restaurant Overnight in Hue DAY 6 : HUE - DANANG - HOI AN ANCIENT TOWN (Breakfast / - ) Departure from Hue to Da Nang.

Arrive Da Nang, we visit the unique museum of Cham Sculpture, which houses the large collection of over 300 stone statues. Afterwards, we move on to Non Nuoc and climb up the steps of Marble Mountains, which are filled with mysterious caves and ancient altars dedicated to mythological deities. Heading to Hoi An Town, recently recognized as a World Heritage Site, we walk its narrow streets with charmingly preserved 200 year old houses, combining the typical architectural styles of Vietnam, China and Japan.

We also see the historical covered bridge, the famous Chinese Pagoda Phuc Kien and the vibrant market on the bank of the Thu Bon River. Overnight in Hoian DAY 7 : DANANG / NHA TRANG (Breakfast / - ) Free at leisure to enjoy sun sea sand at Nhatrang Beach Car transfer to check in hotel in nhatrang and free at leisure to enjoy Nha trang sandy beach.

Overnight in Nhatrang DAY 8 : NHA TRANG (Breakfast / - ) From the hotel in Nha Trang we drive to the Po Nagar Tower, being considered the most beautiful reminder of Cham architecture. We then visit the Pasteur Institute, see Dr. Yesin’s library and office that has been converted into a museum. Later we look at the impressive Long Son Pagoda, tour the famous Oceanographic Institute and admire the beautiful view of Nha Trang coastline from the Hon Chong Rock.

Overnight in Nhatrang DAY 9 : NHA TRANG – DA LAT (Breakfast / - ) Nestled in the majestic and temperate south central highlands of Vietnam, Da Lat has been known for years as the "City of Eternal Spring". We see the beautiful landscapes graced with pine trees , visit the famous Prenn Fall and explore the colorful fruit and vegetable market.

The rest of the day is spent discovering French colonial villas, wandering the Love Valley and pausing at one of Da Lat’s many beautiful flower gardens. Overnight in Dalat DAY 10 : DA LAT / SAIGON (Breakfast / - )
