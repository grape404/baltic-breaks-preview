Nestled in the heart of the Tuscan countryside, between Florence and Bologna, Barberino Designer Outlet is an enchanting village where shopping is a pleasant and relaxing experience. Visiting Barberino Designer Outlet and its 120 stores is a fascinating journey through time and beauty, where the setting of the McArthurGlen Center, inspired by a Tuscan village from the time of the Medici, joins the splendid landscapes and the windows of Italian and international fashion.

In the Centre, you’ll find Italian designer brands for both women and men, international brands, sportswear, and children’s wear. There are highly appealing brands such as Dolce & Gabbana, Blumarine, Hugo Boss, Polo Ralph Lauren, Luisa Spagnoli, Patrizia Pepe, Tommy Hilfiger, Baldinini, Trussardi, Nike and many more. Prices are discounted up to 70% off the retail price all year round
