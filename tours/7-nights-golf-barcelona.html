7 nights BB Hotel Sansi Diputacio 4* Barcelona 5 rounds of golf with green fees and transfers Vist the Iconic Real Club El Prat Pink course, plus CG Vallromanes, CG Montanya, CG Barcelona and CG Llavaneras Arrive in Barcelona and transfer to the excellent city centre Sansi Diputacio 4* hotel, five minutes walk from Las Ramblas and the Plaza Catalunya.

This holiday will give you the chance to play on some of the best golf courses in the Barcelona área. From a hidden gem such as the Golf Montanya in the midst of the Montseny natural park, to the delightful Llavaneras golf club overlooking the Mediterranean sea. And pride of place goes to the championship Pink course at Real Club de golf El Prat.

The combination of great golf and fantastic views, combined with an evening stroll in the vibrant city of Barcelona will make this a golf holiday to treasure. And these are just suggested golf centres – from Barcelona, the PGA Stadium course is a 45 minute drive, and visiting Emporda Golf resort or Golf Perelada is worth considering.

Includes 7 nights BB Hotel Sansi Diputacio 4*. Transfers on arrival and departure, and 5 rounds of golf in Barcelona, with return transfers and Green Fees included. Other hotels and golf courses available on request
